The Politburo awaits your signature, Comrade

This is the real game: the same Python that runs in your terminal, compiled to WebAssembly and running in this tab. Choose how harsh history should be and which decade you inherit, then type your answers below the console. Nothing is installed; nothing is uploaded. v6 adds political capital (i), standing orders (o), a yearly Politburo agenda and a wall clock: when a decision is timed, the countdown appears above the input, and at zero the room moves on without you.
