The Politburo awaits your signature.

This is the real game: the same Python that runs in a terminal, compiled to WebAssembly in this tab. Choose how harsh history should be and which decade you inherit, then answer below the console. The v7 board is a stamped printout; i spends political capital, o sets a standing order, and when a decision is timed the clock answers for you.
