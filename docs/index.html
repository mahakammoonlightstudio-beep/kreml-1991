01 / The appointment

You are not a tank commander. You are a man with a desk.

No map. A board, a directive, a crisis. Each year you choose one public policy, one private habit, and an answer to whatever the decade has already decided to do to you.

October 1964. The telephone on the desk is older than the man answering it. Outside, the country is building tomorrow out of concrete, steel and a plan that has already been corrected twice. Inside, five numbers are falling at a speed nobody has agreed to call a crisis yet.

You have a Politburo that smiles too much, an army that reads the newspapers, a hard-currency vault the oil market keeps borrowing without asking, and four republics whose files are growing thicker than the national ones. Every January, the strongest faction tables a demand. Every December, it is graded.

The Politburo has approved a special account for the next five-year plan. The valas reserve is not on the balance sheet. Nobody in the room finds this strange.

Keep the Union alive until 1991. Do it well and the Politburo offers ten more years: the Red Millennium, 1992–2001. Do it badly and a plenum removes you from the portraits, which is history's way of saying we'll call you.

03 / BETA v8

What changed in the room.

v8 is not a new coat of paint on the terminal. It gives the Politburo faces, gives the ending a dare, and gives the country one more way to run out of time.

01

The room has faces.

Volkov, Sorokina and Balashov send personal memos, ask for favours and remember how you answered. Loyalty is a number now — and the most trusted person quietly returns a point to their own sector every year.

Volkov · Sorokina · Balashov

02

A seed is a dare.

Every run ends with a challenge card: seed, fate, score, difficulty, timeline. Copy it, post it, or hand the same Union to a friend and let them prove they would have done better.

Shareable divergence card

03

Nine winters.

A new 1982 opening begins with three funerals, the Afghan war still bleeding, the shelves already thin and only nine years left on the calendar. The stagnation is warm. The clock is not.

Timeline: THE THREE FUNERALS

04

It fits in a pocket.

The browser edition now frames the transcript with a live situation room: year, five gauges, valas, the court, capital and the year's agenda. On a phone the room folds into a compact strip above the orders.
